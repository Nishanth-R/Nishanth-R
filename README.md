# Hi, I'm Nishanth 👋

I'm the CTO at [Blunav](https://blunav.aero), based in Chennai. We build an operating system for airports. Most of my week goes into deciding what we build next, how the pieces fit together, and whether any of it will hold up on an apron at 5am when three flights are running late.

I also try to be the customer's voice inside the company. If an airport team is annoyed with something we built, I want to hear it before anyone else does.

If you work at an airport, build software or hardware that lives near one, or just have strong opinions about AODBs, I'd like to talk. We would much rather work alongside partners than around them.

## How I got here

Before Blunav I spent about three years at Annalise.ai (part of Harrison.ai) as a technical lead. I worked on their AI marketplace and on getting clinical AI models to plug into hospital infrastructure. Hospitals and airports have more in common than you'd expect. Both run on old systems that can't go down, and both are careful about anything new.

Before that I was at PayPal for almost five years. I worked on an internal ledger that tracked who owned which code assets, and on an internal vector database that helped discover deep rooted issues which we were then to get investment to fix.

Earlier on I was a machine learning engineer at TCS working on text, image and video problems, co-founded a small startup called Tethr.IT where I ran operations, and started my career as a software engineer at iGATE. My degree is in Instrumentation and Control from SRM University.
